# Sync, backup and restore

## iCloud sync

Sign in to iCloud on each device and your logins stay up to date on all of them. Nothing else to set up. **Settings › iCloud Sync** shows whether sync is on.

Your data is stored in your own private iCloud database. Passwords, notes, custom field values and password history are stored encrypted. For end-to-end encryption of all iCloud data, turn on **Advanced Data Protection** in your Apple Account settings.

## Encrypted backups

Sync keeps devices the same, so a mistake syncs too. A backup is a separate copy you can go back to.

### Set up

1. Open **Settings › Encrypted Backup**.
2. Enter a passphrase and tap **Save**.
3. Optionally turn on **Daily Backup to iCloud Drive**.

Write the passphrase down somewhere safe. Without it, a backup cannot be opened by anyone, including you.

### Back up now

Tap **Back Up Now…** and choose where to save the file, for example iCloud Drive.

### Restore

1. Tap **Restore from Backup…** and choose a backup file.
2. Enter the passphrase it was made with.

Restoring merges the backup into your vault:

- Logins you do not have are added.
- Logins where the backup holds a newer version are updated.
- Everything else is left alone. Restoring never deletes anything.

### Backups and app updates

A backup made with any version of the app can be restored on that version or any later one. If you try to restore a backup made by a newer version than the one installed, the app asks you to update first.

Daily backups keep the ten most recent files.
