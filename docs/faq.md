# Questions and answers

**I forgot my backup passphrase. Can it be recovered?**
No. The passphrase is the only key to a backup file. Your synced vault is not affected: set a new passphrase and make a new backup.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your devices and in your private iCloud database. There is no other server.

**Why does a copied password disappear from the clipboard?**
To limit how long it sits there. Change or turn off the delay in **Settings › Clear Clipboard**.

**My logins are not showing on another device.**
Check that both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account and that **Settings › iCloud Sync** says **On**. The first sync on a new device can take a few minutes.

**Password Manager does not appear when I sign in to a website.**
Turn it on in the system Settings under **General › AutoFill & Passwords**.

**Can I edit logins on my Apple Watch?**
No. The watch app is for looking things up.

**I deleted a login by mistake.**
Open **Trash** in the sidebar and restore it. Items stay there for 30 days.

**What happens to my items if I delete a folder?**
They are kept and move to the top level. Subfolders of the deleted folder are removed.

**How deep can folders go?**
Three levels: a folder, a subfolder, and one more inside that.

**Does it support one-time codes?**
Not in this version.
